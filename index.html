Vidura

Vidura is a private, personal assistant system built and run by one person, for that person's own use. It is not a product, is not offered to the public, and has no users other than its operator.

It reads material its operator gives it access to, keeps what it learns on its operator's own computer, and helps with everyday organisation and decision-making.

This page exists because a personal application that connects to a Google account is required to have one.
